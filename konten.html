STORY OF US

HOW WE MET

Our story began in the most unexpected way — through a mutual connection during one of those warm, rainy evenings that make Jakarta feel magical. A shared laugh over something trivial turned into hours of conversation, and what followed was a friendship built on honesty, laughter, and understanding. We discovered a shared love for travel, good food, and quiet moments. Looking back, it feels like the universe had been quietly arranging this all along.

HOW WE PROPOSE

After two years of adventures and countless memories, the proposal came in the most intimate way — just the two of us, overlooking the sunset at our favourite place. With trembling hands and a full heart, the question was asked and answered with joyful tears. It was a moment frozen in time, a beginning wrapped in gratitude and love.
